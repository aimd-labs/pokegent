## Executive decision

Use PokéBot as a Gen-3 mechanism and scenario catalog. Keep Pokegent's typed decision frame, single-writer actor, one registered executor, current-snapshot revalidation, dual bounded waits, postconditions, objective revisions, public/private data policy and fail-closed rules. PokéBot's generator-driven control loop/listeners and deterministic strategy routines demonstrate mature local interactions, but they are not a drop-in agent runtime or complete campaign planner. Its global context, memory-writing/debug interfaces and some unbounded retry/wait loops violate or do not prove Pokegent's execution invariants. Do not import them or use their timeout constants as proof. No module-for-module rewrite.

## A. Navigation and runtime boundaries

### A1. Exact world state and variants (#139)

[ROM identity and family predicates](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/roms.py#L78-L174), [symbol selection](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/game.py#L28-L187), [save-block handling](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/memory.py#L118-L218), [task/script context](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/tasks.py#L14-L176), [frame-cache age/reset](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/state_cache.py#L21-L112) and [frame-loop reset and dispatch](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/main.py#L76-L142) show why revision, language and family cannot be inferred from a file extension or title. The reference has global game-context and family branches; copy neither their tables nor this pattern wholesale. Keep per-ROM hash + schema admission, and make adapter-owned reader/capabilities/map decoding explicit. The existing [Pokegent profile catalog](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/blob/c73774285e5e164d01629ca14a5e8e551e03493f/pokegent/references/rom_profiles.yaml) refuses Rev 1; do not enable it by assuming Rev 0 offsets. Follow #139: FireRed revisions and LeafGreen, then Emerald, then Gen1/2. Prove one second game uses core contracts without new core gameplay branches; add independent negative fixtures for wrong ROM, language, revision, invalid pointer/decoder, and restore identity.

### A2. Local paths, global routes, and interrupts (#180, #162, #133, #178, #106)

The [reference local planner](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/map_path.py#L620-L764) handles connected-map coordinates, direction/elevation access, live object's current/previous position and movement capabilities. Its own contract excludes arbitrary multi-warp paths and Fly; a warp may be the terminal destination. [Warp decoding](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/map.py#L525-L570) distinguishes special dynamic destinations and missing paired warp IDs. [Walking execution](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/util/walking.py#L115-L225) observes movement readiness, facing and waypoint timeouts; [replanning](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/util/walking.py#L314-L406) recalculates after timeout or stops for an unexpected script. Its retry loop is not a suitable global bound.

Pokegent already has [world graph](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/blob/c73774285e5e164d01629ca14a5e8e551e03493f/pokegent/navigation/graph.py), [route adapter](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/blob/c73774285e5e164d01629ca14a5e8e551e03493f/pokegent/navigation/adapter.py#L780-L839), movement macros and typed ownership. Keep those and the #106 offline materialization plan. Separate:
1. Immutable map topology and proven gated transitions (local ROM evidence).
2. Current occupancy, movement mode, direction, elevation and dynamic destination (fresh RAM).
3. Plan edge, exact legal option, executor step, actual landing and outcome (same-frame identity).
4. Bounded retry/replan and durable progress (verified effect, not position change alone).

For Seafoam, obtain a sanitized native capture of the B2F entry and expected B1F landing, facing/tile behavior, active current/boulder/warp state, chosen option, frame and actual outcome. Test direction-specific warp, dynamic/unknown destination, blocked NPC, interrupted battle, forced movement, repeated map/edge cycle and refusal on stale state. No source inspection proves the reported warp itself or its cause. Do not suppress every edge forever after a transient failure: attempts must be scoped to current topology/capability/occupancy identity and budgets. #178's [PR #179](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/pull/179) is a symptom-level fix according to its description; a visible route step must not imply a non-offered option is executable. Verify correspondence before claiming route legality, with payload leak safeguards intact. #162 is a smaller independent no-objective local move offering, not a global explore-policy refactor.

### A3. Fly is present but too narrowly offered (#181, #168)

The reference [fly action](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/util/higher_level_actions.py#L50-L107) checks badge, visited flag, carrier and region; its [region-map reader](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/region_map.py#L1-L32) reads live cursor state. The issue's suggested eight-badge requirement is wrong for FireRed; existing [Fly capability](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/blob/c73774285e5e164d01629ca14a5e8e551e03493f/pokegent/actions/field/fly.py#L117-L172) already uses Thunder Badge. Existing [FieldBuilder](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/blob/c73774285e5e164d01629ca14a5e8e551e03493f/pokegent/actions/field/builder.py#L2033-L2096) exposes Fly when a semantic route edge or explicit map destination selects it and launch-cell proof holds. Existing [route preference](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/blob/c73774285e5e164d01629ca14a5e8e551e03493f/pokegent/actions/field/fly.py#L174-L209) includes a minimum travel saving. The explicit source says live profile does not yet expose sFlyMap and only proven start cells are admitted.

These are concrete candidate causes of missing offering, not a diagnosis of the reported run. Don't add a duplicate executor or lower safety. Independently verify cursor/task fields against pinned pret source and local Rev 0. Distinguish available-but-not-offered, unknown launch, missing destination data/visited flag, illegal indoor state, explicit objective forbid and valid route preference. Test legal no-goal and route-to-non-Fly-destination cases, indoor/unvisited/missing-badge/missing-carrier/unknown cursor refusals, resume/cancel, exact arrival and bounded travel. If a general Fly choice is allowed, limit it with stable pagination/ranking rather than an unbounded list; do not mark it serves_goal unless proved.

### A4. Fishing is the smallest high-value new mechanic (#176)

[Reference fishing mode](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/fishing.py#L13-L51) selects a rod, registers it if necessary, and invokes the fishing routine. [Task-phase action](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/util/higher_level_actions.py#L109-L150) and [listener](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/_listeners.py#L309-L343) distinguish bite/response and encounter, got-away and unsuccessful outcomes. This routine advances a generator step, not an atomic complete fishing transaction. [Pokegent coverage](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/blob/c73774285e5e164d01629ca14a5e8e551e03493f/pokegent/actions/coverage.py#L217-L232) explicitly lists fishing as REAL_GAP. [Wild table](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/blob/c73774285e5e164d01629ca14a5e8e551e03493f/pokegent/games/gen3/firered/rom/wild.py) already models method/rate/level. Don't copy the upstream phase enum or raw state numbers; derive a validated Rev 0 task decoder from independent source and local fixtures.

Proposed acceptance contract (not frozen schema):
- Build: offer only existing rod(s) and verified legal facing/target/readiness; separate item registration from fishing intent if needed. Unknown water/rod/task ownership stays unknown.
- Execute: single writer, choose once, verify fresh evidence, select registered rod or bag path, observe bounded task phases; only react on verified bite/current owned prompt. Pause/replan for other scene owners. No generic A/B spamming, no LLM call for mechanical timing.
- Outcome: successful execution can legitimately mean no bite or got away. Distinguish typed game outcome from rejected/timed_out/uncertain infrastructure action; keep existing ActionResult contract. Verify battle transition when hooked or safe field return otherwise. Don't infer capture or dex progress from a bite.
- Tests: missing rod, wrong tile, occupied/unknown/busy state, stale decision, all supported rods, no bite, got away, hooked battle, unexpected scene, task timeout, held input release and next frame. Add private Route 25 scenario after synthetic tests; no publicly bundled saves. Verify national species IDs (issue mixes Poliwag/Poliwhirl) and method using exact ROM data.

### A5. Ordinary encounter mechanics and dex planning

[Raw/effective encounter readers](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/map.py#L1826-L2019), [repel/ability calculations](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/map.py#L2028-L2299) and [unit tests](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/tests/test_map.py#L102-L334) are a key reference for distinguishing nominal slot rates, level ranges, lead ability and repel effects; not every probability is an observed outcome. [Sweet Scent mode](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/sweet_scent.py#L9-L28) uses a normal field move. [Feebas helper](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/fishing.py#L48-L85) models RSE special tiles; the upstream README states its default mode searches rather than peeks. Respect player-knowledge policy: don't silently expose hidden spawn/secret data. Add a bounded Sweet Scent core action with current carrier/PP, terrain and game-specific restrictions, verified encounter or normal failure. Validate repel level, duplicate slots, zero encounters, title-specific ability effects and normalized distributions; no claims based on generic data alone.

Current [DexFallback](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/blob/c73774285e5e164d01629ca14a5e8e551e03493f/pokegent/goals/dex_fallback.py#L19-L68) considers unowned dex 1–151, only land encounters, and simple local/probability cost with full-HP and ball-count gates; this is deliberately limited and not full Pokédex completion. Build a title/revision-scoped acquisition graph incrementally: wild methods, gifts, static, evolution, breeding, NPC/link trades, version exclusives, event restrictions and unavailable/unknown routes. Keep evidence of the prerequisite and acquisition action distinct. Offer candidate goals and factual costs to the agent; no silently hardcoded living-dex policy. Test a species with multiple methods, fishing-only target, version-exclusive/unavailable target, missing prerequisites, evolution from owned parent, unknown encounter facts and completed goal. Do not mark every National Dex entry obtainable in one cartridge.

## B. Battle, party, breeding, and normal side content

[Battle state](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/battle_state.py#L221-L467), [strategy interface](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/battle_strategies/_interface.py#L71-L186), [calculation helpers](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/battle_strategies/_util.py#L165-L295) and [catch strategy](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/battle_strategies/catch.py#L41-L124) reveal explicit doubles targets, distinct Safari action sets, speed/damage/catch/escape facts and deterministic strategies. Pokegent already has battle builders, legality, damage/effect rules and exchange models. Compare assumptions and differential vectors with independent game source plus MIT Showdown/damage-calc in R06. Preserve agent choice: no forced strongest move; include target, immunity, trapping, resource cost, uncertainty, recoil/status and partner impact when evidenced. Test distinct active battlers/targets, stale party slot, forced switch, item refusal, impossible catch and Safari limits. No claims of full formula or battle-format coverage.

[Reference Pokémon reader](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/pokemon.py#L871-L1122) and [derived traits](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/pokemon.py#L1240-L1310) show individual identity, nature, gender, shiny, Hidden Power and related state. Pokegent [mon decoder](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/blob/c73774285e5e164d01629ca14a5e8e551e03493f/pokegent/games/gen3/firered/reader/mon.py#L203-L295) already verifies checksum and reads personality, OT, EVs/IVs, friendship, egg bit. Do not implement a second decoder. Validate a derived shiny status and traits on known synthetic inputs with version-specific rules; unreadable or invalid state stays unknown. Don't expose secret trainer ID or egg contents by default; use player-knowledge visibility boundaries, ensure event deduplication on resume. Shiny detection is **core**; repeated hunting is a policy add-on.

[Party/PC](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/pokemon_party.py#L14-L101), [storage](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/pokemon_storage.py#L60-L139) and [PC operation types](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/util/pc_interaction.py#L27-L87) motivate stable-identity deposit/withdraw, held item and capacity checks. Upstream release handling and farming policies are not our authorization to release anything. Verify identity, exact storage and item/party deltas; keep last healthy Pokémon and irreversible intent safeguards. Existing Pokegent PC, move/evolution and shop builders remain the starting point. Upstream [evolution](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/tests/test_battle_evolution.py) and [move-learning](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/tests/test_battle_move_learning.py) tests include allow/deny, empty-slot, replacement and unchanged-moves cases. For #165 use the already merged recovery and actual owner confirmation; do not duplicate its worker.

[EV training](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/ev_train.py#L24-L53), [target and resource checks](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/ev_train.py#L105-L198), [level grinding](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/level_grind.py#L23-L114) and [battle rotation](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/battle_strategies/_util.py#L522-L599) provide scenario inspiration, not a complete team optimizer. Pokegent's [team-planning facts](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/blob/c73774285e5e164d01629ca14a5e8e551e03493f/pokegent/games/gen3/mechanics/team_planning.py) already calculate matchup/speed/level-related facts; extend with explicit training requirements, EV yield/limits, ability/held-item modifiers, encounter probability, damage distribution, moveset tradeoffs, PC candidates and resource cost. Core exposes factual alternatives; the model/agent chooses objective. Native tests should check observed gains and caps, not just predicted deltas.

[Daycare compatibility](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/daycare.py#L9-L142), [egg collection/hatching mode](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/daycare.py#L64-L288) and [hatch listener](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/_listeners.py#L367-L435) are a strong reference for an ordinary breeding capability. Separate inspection, parent compatibility, deposit/withdraw, collect and hatch; check FireRed daycare unlock and capacities. No auto-release of unwanted Pokémon. Normal Safari, roaming encounters, legendary puzzle steps, berry interaction, minigames and transport are also core/game adapter scope. [Puzzle solver](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/puzzle_solver.py), [Safari mode](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/safari.py) and [roamer mode](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/roamer_reencounter.py) are selected scenario references, not full campaign/postgame proof. [Pokegent coverage](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/blob/c73774285e5e164d01629ca14a5e8e551e03493f/pokegent/actions/coverage.py) marks several ordinary mechanics not needed for E4; that is a campaign disposition, not a permanent exclusion from core. No complete link trading, dex UI, main-story or postgame coverage established in this R02 inspection.

## C. Add-on and event interface

[BotPlugin hooks](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/plugin_interface.py#L12-L206) provide battle-start/end, visible encounter, egg, evolution, pickup, whiteout and filtering events. [Plugin dispatch](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/plugins.py#L21-L181) permits custom Python modules/generators; do not import that authority model. Pokegent [addon docs](https://github.com/aimd-labs/pokegent/blob/c73774285e5e164d01629ca14a5e8e551e03493f/docs/addons.md) describe stream, dashboard and System One, not a general policy API.

Proposed future extension split: read-only, redacted observer subscriptions (telemetry/OBS/Discord); separately admitted policy extensions that propose objectives or choose currently offered options. Capabilities and required game/revision support are declared; no raw RAM write, input port or arbitrary new executor access. Hooks must be bounded, failure-isolated, with versioned event contracts and single writer. Core does not depend on external services. An opt-in shiny policy can use ordinary encounter/rod/egg/static/reset actions, target filter, max attempts/time, safe stop on target, capture/resource safeguards, optional checkpoint and notification. No reset or release without explicit policy authorization and verified state; resets must preserve evidence and avoid duplicate encounters. RNG manipulation is separately opt-in; [reference memory writes](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/memory.py#L72-L82) and [RNG helpers](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/modes/util/soft_reset.py#L139-L175) are not allowed in normal gameplay. [Shiny test](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/tests/test_mode_spin.py#L31-L61) uses precomputed RNG setup; test instrumentation is not a production policy.

[Encounter logging](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/encounter.py#L86-L209) and [stats database](https://github.com/40Cakes/pokebot-gen3/blob/f14ab79db960315db02333928ba444f9d425b6fc/modules/stats.py#L591-L825) motivate typed encounter/fishing events, stable deduplication, attempt/outcome counters, phase summaries and resume identity. Build on Pokegent's existing EventEnvelope and run ledger; don't add a separate DB. This is also useful in all three decision modes. Avoid public raw mon bytes or private save paths; capture and expose only reviewed safe fields.

Build independent synthetic fixtures and private state manifests (exact ROM/revision, backend build, start-state hash, legal intent, expected terminal state, frame/wall bounds and redacted outcome evidence). Repeated native acceptance under #135/#138 is separate from public ROM-free CI. Keep real ROMs, extracted game data, screenshots, saves and traces outside the public repo.
