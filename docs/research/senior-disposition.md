# Senior disposition of the research stack

Date: 2026-11-24. This note records what the twelve research packages change and what they do
not authorize. It is non-normative; runtime contracts and maintainer review still decide.

## Accepted principles

- **FireRed first.** The existing FireRed Rev0 evidence path stays the working baseline until a
  second exact title is admitted on its own evidence.
- **Local exact truth.** ROM identity, profile, backend, revision and scene facts come from the
  local admitted artifacts and current observations, not from name matching or reference prose.
- **Current executor.** One writer, the existing legal-action executor and post-action
  verification stay authoritative. Research does not add a second input path.
- **System One is empirically gated.** Routing and confidence ideas remain proposals until
  measured on frozen cases; no threshold is adopted as calibrated policy.
- **Exact profiles.** Support is reported per exact game, region/revision, backend and host.
  Boot or readable memory is not title support.

## Not accepted, or deferred

- No framework rewrite and no new architecture solely from these notes.
- No raw actions, direct RAM writes or compatibility facades that bypass legality.
- No copying of unlicensed, GPL/AGPL or noncommercial code, data or assets. License signals are
  not clearance.
- No blanket confidence score or model-based input authority.
- DS, GamePlugin and add-on work stay deferred behind FireRed acceptance gates and their own
  reviewed implementation. DS research supplies evidence only; it does not choose a backend.
- #106 remains **PAUSED**; this stack does not reopen it.

## Status language

Acceptance of a research package means the written findings are usable for planning. It is not a
support claim, an implementation commitment, a benchmark result or a release gate. Acceptance
tests named in the packages are proposed until executed and reviewed.
